Model assumptions

Teaching model with realistic numbers, not a full furnace simulation.

Growth rate from the interface heat balance ksGs − klGl = ρL·vg. Pulling faster than vg raises the meniscus, which narrows the crystal.

Thin crystals radiate heat away more easily: Gs = G(1 + c/R).

Gradient is 12 % steeper at the edge than at the centre. Defect-free means v/G within ±12 % of 0.13 mm²/(min·K).

Melt level fixed (crucible is lifted), no rotation, oxygen or dopants. Time runs far faster than real life.
