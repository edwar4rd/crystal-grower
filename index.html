How do pull rate and temperature gradient shape a growing silicon crystal?

A seed crystal is dipped into 1414 °C molten silicon and slowly pulled up. The crystal that follows is only as good as the conditions at its growing edge.

  1. A thin neck removes dislocations
  2. Diameter is a heat balance
  3. Defects depend on v/G

1The thin neck

Dipping the seed causes thermal shock, which creates dislocations. Pull fast at first: the crystal gets thin and the dislocations escape through its surface.

Neck diameter:  ·  gradient G in neck:

Why can a thin crystal be pulled fast? It loses heat from its sides more easily, so G is steeper (see card 2).
